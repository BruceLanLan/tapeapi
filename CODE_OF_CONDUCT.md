# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

> English is authoritative. 中文见下半部分，章节编号一一对应。
> A short summary in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## 1. Our pledge

We want taking part in TapeAPI — issues, pull requests, reviews, specification discussions and any other project
space — to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their background, identity, experience or language.
English and Chinese are both welcome here.

## 2. Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, and assume good faith.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people. Give and accept review gracefully.
- Keep technical claims checkable: point to the test, the TAP section or the transaction.
- Respect people's time, privacy and security: do not post someone's personal data, keys or private messages.

## 3. Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or sexualised language and imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, and deliberate derailing.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Scams, phishing, impersonating a maintainer or a service, or promoting tokens or investments in project spaces.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## 4. Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.

## 5. Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers privately: contact **@BruceLanLan** on GitHub, or, if the matter must stay
confidential, use the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/BruceLanLan/tapeapi/security/advisories/new)
and say in the title that it is a conduct report, not a vulnerability. Reports are handled in confidence.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn,
temporarily ban or permanently ban a participant, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.

## 1. 我们的承诺

我们希望每个人在参与 TapeAPI 时——issue、PR、评审、规范讨论以及任何其它项目空间——都不受骚扰，无论其背景、身份、
经验或语言。这里中文与英文都欢迎。

## 2. 期望的行为

- 友善、耐心，默认对方出于善意。
- 批评想法与代码，而不是人。得体地给出和接受评审。
- 技术论断要可核查：指向测试、TAP 章节或交易。
- 尊重他人的时间、隐私与安全：不要公开他人的个人信息、密钥或私信。

## 3. 不可接受的行为

- 骚扰、侮辱、威胁，或带性意味的言语与图像。
- 人身或政治攻击、钓鱼挑衅、故意带偏话题。
- 未经明确许可公开他人的私人信息。
- 诈骗、钓鱼、冒充维护者或服务，或在项目空间推销代币与投资。
- 其它在专业场合中理性的人会认为不当的行为。

## 4. 适用范围

适用于所有项目空间，以及代表本项目公开发言的场合。

## 5. 报告与执行

请私下向维护者报告：在 GitHub 上联系 **@BruceLanLan**；若需保密，可使用仓库的
[私密报告表单](https://github.com/BruceLanLan/tapeapi/security/advisories/new)，并在标题中注明这是行为准则报告而非漏洞。
报告会被保密处理。

维护者可以编辑或删除违反本准则的评论、提交、issue 及其它贡献，并视行为的严重程度与反复程度给予警告、临时禁止或
永久禁止参与。
