这里检查什么What this page checks

你的浏览器直接请求两个服务的健康检查接口，显示是否在线、往返延迟、服务用来签名回答的地址（signer），以及持有人委托的到期时间。委托一旦过期，客户端会拒绝该服务的每一个回答，因此剩余不足 14 天时这里会标黄。 Your browser asks each service's health endpoint directly and shows whether it answers, the round-trip time, the address it signs answers with (signer), and when the holder's delegation expires. Once a delegation lapses, clients reject every answer from that service, so fewer than 14 days left is flagged here.

更深的检查在仓库的监控任务里定时运行，大约每 30 分钟一次（GitHub 可能推迟定时任务）：像客户端一样从 BNB Chain 解析服务（circuit 11 与 12，处理器 1013），核对链上清单里的 signer 与服务实际使用的一致，并做一次经过验签的真实调用。 A deeper check runs in the repository's monitor, scheduled about every 30 minutes (GitHub may delay scheduled runs): it resolves each service from BNB Chain exactly as a client does (circuits 11 and 12 of processor 1013), checks that the signer in the on-chain manifest is the one the service uses, and makes one real call whose signed answer is verified.
