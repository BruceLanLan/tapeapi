The Receipt Layer for AI

What one receipt proves (TAPI-21 AI usage receipts)

问题Question 回执里的依据Evidence in the receipt 证明Proven

回执不证明实际跑的是哪个模型。服务方可以把便宜模型的回答标成贵的。签名带来的是可追责：回执无法抵赖，任何人用抽检探针发测试请求、公开结果，都会留下证据。

A receipt does not prove which model actually ran. A provider could label a cheaper model's answer as a dearer one. What the signature adds is accountability: a receipt cannot be disowned, so anyone running spot checks and publishing the results leaves evidence.

  • 价目表钉在链上每个模型、每种币一个价，改价要持有人重新发布

你照旧经营：接口、密钥、渠道和计费都不变。旁路放在你的网关前面，所以回执覆盖的正是你向用户收取的用量与价格。

Your business runs as before: your API, keys, channels and billing stay the same. The sidecar sits in front of your gateway, so a receipt covers exactly the usage and price you charge your users.

价目表里的价格只是公开的声明，不经过结算：用户照旧向你付款，回执里的金额是一笔可以核对的声明。

Prices in the table are published, not settled: your users pay you as they do today, and the amount in a receipt is a claim anyone can check.

TapeAPI 只面向在上游服务商条款范围内合规经营的服务方。身份、价目表在链上，换域名、换服务器，用户跟着链上记录走；协议不提供、也不帮助绕开上游的封禁或地区限制。

TapeAPI is for providers operating within their upstream providers' terms. Identity and prices live on chain, so users follow the on-chain record when you change domain or server; the protocol does not offer, or help with, getting around an upstream's bans or regional limits.

One Identity, Three More Layers

MCP

签名的 MCP 工具

Signed MCP tools

MCP 的工具结果是服务器随口给的，工具定义也可以在你批准之后被偷偷改掉。TapeAPI 让每个结果带签名和回执，把工具定义的哈希钉在链上。公共端点直接用；自己的服务器加签名代理；本地命令 tapeapi-mcp 在模型看到结果之前先核验。

In MCP a tool result is whatever the server sent, and a tool definition can change after you approved it. TapeAPI signs every result, attaches a receipt and pins the hash of the tool definitions on chain. Use the public endpoint, put the signing proxy in front of your own server, or run tapeapi-mcp locally to check every answer before the model sees it.

TAPI-26 · TAPI-27

私密通道与群聊

Private channels and groups

两个容器之间的端到端加密通道：双向认证、前向保密、防重放和乱序。群聊最多 32 个容器（实验性的格式 2 最多 128 个），群主管理成员，每条消息单独签名。载体可以是公共中继、你自己的中继或链上的 ChannelBus，都只看到密文。

End-to-end encrypted channels between two containers: mutual authentication, forward secrecy, replay and reorder protection. Groups of up to 32 containers (up to 128 in the experimental format 2), with an owner who manages members and a signature on every message. Carried by the public relay, your own relay or ChannelBus on chain, each of which sees ciphertext only.

CHAINS

多链：BNB Chain、X Layer、Base

Multi-chain: BNB Chain, X Layer, Base

跟随 TapeOut 上了 X Layer 和 Base。名字带区号，比如 1.2.230.tape 是 X Layer 上处理器 230 的 1 号。身份、解析、回执和 MCP 核验都能用；支付只会在 BNB Chain。

TapeAPI follows TapeOut to X Layer and Base. Names carry an area code: 1.2.230.tape is circuit 1 of processor 230 on X Layer. Identity, resolution, receipts and MCP checks all work there; payments will be on BNB Chain only.

隐私：能保护什么，不能保护什么

Privacy: What Is Protected and What Is Not

受保护的

Protected

  • 通道与群聊的内容端到端加密，中继和链上只有密文
  • Channel and group contentEnd-to-end encrypted; relays and the chain hold ciphertext only
  • AI 回执只带哈希不含提示词和回答；经 SDK 或 tapeapi-verify 发出的请求默认追加 128 位随机空白，短提示词没法靠猜哈希对上
  • AI receipts carry hashes onlyNo prompt, no answer; requests sent through the SDK or tapeapi-verify get 128 random bits of whitespace by default, so a short prompt cannot be confirmed by hashing guesses
  • MCP 核验链接只带哈希不带参数和结果；参数很短时（一个地址、一个编号）仍可能被猜出
  • MCP links carry hashes onlyNot the params or the result; very short params (an address, a token ID) can still be guessed
  • 链上通道读取SDK 的 busPrivacyReader（实验性）默认读整个 ChannelBus、在本地筛选，节点看不出你的房间
  • Reading channels on chainThe SDK's busPrivacyReader (experimental) reads all of ChannelBus by default and filters locally, so nodes cannot see your rooms
  • 日志最小化我们的公共服务在配置里关闭了逐请求调用日志；Cloudflare 自己的请求记录是否含 IP 仍待核实
  • Minimal logsOur public services' configuration turns per-request invocation logs off; whether Cloudflare's own records include the IP is still to be verified

没有多数决。三家里两家一致、一家不同，默认结果是拒绝。多数决在三取二时可被两家合谋伪造，而拒绝只会让调用失败。调用方可以显式降级，但那是它自己的选择。

There is no majority vote. Two of three agreeing with one dissenting is a rejection by default. A majority rule can be forged by two colluding providers out of three; a rejection only denies. A caller may opt into the weaker mode explicitly, but that is its own decision.

No mandatory protocol fee; a default 1% maintenance contribution that any provider can turn off; the operator has no fee switch. The 1% comes out of the provider's share only when a paid channel settles, and the user's price does not change. The paid-call escrow is not deployed, so no call is charged today. TapeAPI has no token.

实验性：付费通道

Experimental: Paid Channels

EXPERIMENTAL · NOT DEPLOYED TAPI-22 · 不在 1.0 的稳定承诺内 · 部署前须经独立审计 TAPI-22 · outside the 1.0 stability promise · audited before any deployment

付费调用的设计：调用方离线签发累计凭证，钱进一条只属于这个服务的托管通道，服务方攒够了再上链结算一次，调用本身不花 gas。合约代码和测试在仓库里，但没有部署；下面的数字是本地实测，只说明这个设计的成本。

The design for paid calls: the caller signs cumulative vouchers off chain into an escrow channel that belongs to that service alone, and the provider settles once on chain when it is worth doing, so a call itself costs no gas. The contracts and their tests are in the repository but not deployed; the figures below are local measurements that only show what the design costs.
