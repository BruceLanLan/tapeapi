New in 2.0: Standards by Default

DEFAULT SINCE 2.0 TAP-10 v1.1 §3–§7 · TAP-11 §2（Draft）· conform: false 弃用，2.x 内保留 TAP-10 v1.1 §3–§7 · TAP-11 §2 (Draft) · conform: false deprecated, kept in 2.x

2.0 只有一个主题：我们向 TapeOut 官方 TAP 流程提交的规则，以及 TapeOut 自己规定的规则，就是你什么都不传时 SDK 的行为。1.4 起这些规则以可选的 conform: 'tap10' 提供；2.0 把它变成默认。线上格式一个字节都不变：2.0 的客户端照样核验所有 1.x 的清单、委托、签名回答和回执。TAP-10 与 TAP-11 还是 Draft，默认按一条规则跟随它们的文本：放宽与新增状态名在 2.x 次版本里生效，收紧先作为可选 profile、默认到 3.0 才切。一个已知例外：不开 allChains 时，形如 0x…#4246 的"处理器合约#ID"会被拒绝（data.status: 'unsupported'，这是 TapeAPI 自己的名字），而 TAP-11 §2.1 要求接受它；传 allChains: true 即按 TAP-10 §4.1 读遍所有链解析（会同时访问 Base 与 X Layer 的节点）。

2.0 has one theme: what we submitted to TapeOut's official TAP process, and what TapeOut itself specifies, is what the SDK does when you pass nothing. Since 1.4 these rules were the opt-in conform: 'tap10'; 2.0 makes them the default. No wire format changes: a 2.0 client verifies every 1.x manifest, delegation, signed response and receipt. TAP-10 and TAP-11 are Drafts, so the default follows their text by one rule: a loosening or a new status name in a 2.x minor release, a tightening first as an optional profile and the default in 3.0. One known exception: without allChains, a processor contract#ID such as 0x…#4246 is refused (data.status: 'unsupported', a name of TapeAPI's own), where TAP-11 §2.1 says a client must accept it; pass allChains: true to resolve it on every chain as TAP-10 §4.1 says (this also queries the nodes of Base and X Layer).

01.1

实验性：有界代理

Experimental: Bounded Agents

EXPERIMENTAL · NOT DEPLOYED @tapeapi/sdk/agent · 托管合约未审计、未部署 · 主网不可用 · 不受 2.x 兼容承诺约束 @tapeapi/sdk/agent · escrow not audited, not deployed · not on mainnet · outside the 2.x compatibility promise

1.7 的阶段 0 只记录谁可以替哪个容器做哪件任务，什么都不强制执行。有界代理是阶段 1：代理可以替容器花钱，而容器最多会亏多少，由托管合约算死、可在链上核对。电路持有人为代理可以付费的每个服务各充值一条托管通道，把一把只为这份授权书生成的钥匙授权为这些通道上的会话钥匙，有效期到授权书到期。钥匙泄露或代理作恶，损失最多是放进这些通道的钱，而且只能付给授权书点名的服务。

Phase 0 (1.7) records who may act for a container and on which task, and enforces nothing. Bounded agents are phase 1: an agent may spend for a container, and the most the container can lose is fixed by an escrow contract and checkable on chain. The circuit's holder funds one escrow channel per service the agent may pay, and authorises a key made for that one mandate as a session key on those channels until the mandate expires. A leaked or misbehaving agent key can cost at most what was put in those channels, and only toward the services the mandate names.

  • 核验{ enforcement: 'escrow-channel' }：在同一个钉块上读每条有上限的通道，全部通过才报告“已强制执行”；链上限住的始终是通道余额
  • The check{ enforcement: 'escrow-channel' }: every capped channel is read at one pinned block, and the mandate is reported enforced only when all pass; what the chain bounds is always the channel's balance
  • 持有人的计划holderPlan 从链上读事实，给出未签名的 approve、fund、authorizeSession；afterPlan 读回核对，closeOut 取回剩余；会话历史防止重复充值
  • The holder's planholderPlan reads its facts from the chain and returns the unsigned approve, fund and authorizeSession; afterPlan reads them back, closeOut withdraws the rest; a session history stops a key being funded twice
  • 累计限额器createSpendLimiter 绝不签出累计超过“开始时已结算额加上限”的凭证；默认拒绝单次超过上限 10% 的调用
  • A cumulative limitercreateSpendLimiter never signs a voucher past what was settled at the start plus the cap; by default it refuses one call above 10% of the cap
  • 重启后接着处理订单会话钥匙与订单记录用口令保险箱加密保存（scrypt 与 XChaCha20-Poly1305）；resume 只恢复仍有效的订单，已停止、撤销或过期的绝不恢复
  • Orders resume after a restartSession keys and order records are kept sealed by a passphrase vault (scrypt and XChaCha20-Poly1305); resume continues only live orders, never a stopped, revoked or expired one
  • 交付里的钱交付可以附代理签名的花费声明，与链上读数和回执逐项对账
  • Money in a deliveryA delivery may carry the agent's signed spend statement, reconciled against the chain and the receipts

今天能验证的只有本地：在已安装依赖的仓库检出根目录，第一条在 SDK 的假链上跑一单完整的花费流程（含泄露钥匙、涨价、换托管等对抗用例），第二条在本地的托管合约副本上跑同样的交易。不联网、不碰真实钱包、不花钱。What can be checked today is local only. At the root of a checkout with its dependencies installed, the first runs one spend order end to end on the SDK's fake chain (with a leaked key, a price rise and an escrow change among the adversarial cases); the second runs the same kind of transactions against a local copy of the escrow contract. No network, no real wallet, no cost.

Two More Experiments

  • 美元报价、BEM 结算 · TAPI-22 §3.6TAP 草稿 #52（尚未合并的拉取请求）的参考实现。清单可以用美元定价；服务方在签名报价里给出 BEM 汇率，客户端签凭证前对照链上参考池核对，每个收费回答都附签名的收费记录；priceBEM 仍是单次最多花多少。没有 usdPricing 的清单逐字节照旧。托管合约未部署，所以不收费

  • Reviews between containers · @tapeapi/sdk/reviewThe reference implementation of the TAP draft #54 (an open pull request). 没有多数决。三家里两家一致、一家不同，默认结果是拒绝。多数决在三取二时可被两家合谋伪造，而拒绝只会让调用失败。调用方可以显式降级，但那是它自己的选择。
